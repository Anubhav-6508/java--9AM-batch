# Online Gaming Leaderboard — Scenario-Based Problem



A product-based company is developing an online multiplayer gaming platform.

Players earn points by completing missions. The platform must maintain a leaderboard that automatically reflects player rankings whenever their scores change.

Each player contains:

- Player ID
- Player Name
- Score
- Level

Business Requirements

1. Every player must have a unique Player ID, and the same player must never appear twice in the leaderboard.

2. Players must always be arranged from highest score to lowest score.

3. If two players have the same score, the player with the higher level must appear first. If both score and level are equal, arrange them alphabetically by player name.

4. Whenever a player's score or level changes, the leaderboard must immediately reflect the updated ranking.

5. The system must retrieve the top 10 players and allow finding the immediate higher-ranked and lower-ranked players without manually sorting the entire leaderboard.

6. If a player leaves the game permanently, their record must be removed and the leaderboard must automatically adjust.

### Additional Condition

The leaderboard must maintain its correct ranking whenever players are added, updated, or removed.
